Meet Tyra Young

About: AI Had Me at Hello

I thought I owed the IRS a substantial amount in taxes, so I did what a lot of people do: I paid a tax relief company about $600 to handle it.

Then something told me, 'Call the IRS yourself.' Now, I've spent HOURS on hold with the IRS before. But that day, I got right through. I asked what my balance was, and the lady said, 'Zero.'

ZERO. I was paying somebody to resolve a debt I didn't even have.

I called the company for my money back, and they told me no refunds. So I uploaded their contract to AI and asked, 'Can you find a way for me to get my money back?' It reviewed the contract and told me exactly what to say. I said it word for word, and they refunded me RIGHT THEN AND THERE. No ten-day wait. Didn't have to pass GO. DONE.

I sat there thinking, 'Oh, it's ON AND POPPING!'

That was my 'AI had me at hello' moment. In one afternoon, AI helped me understand a contract, speak up for myself, and put $600 back in my pocket. That's when I saw what was possible.

After decades as a small business owner, as a nail technician, carpenter, life coach, and in logistics, I know what it's like to wear every hat and still feel behind. Now my team and I bring solutions to America's entrepreneurs, business owners, and everyday people, so AI can work for them the way it worked for me.

Tyra Young, Founder & AI Strategist Connect with Tyra on LinkedIn and Facebook
